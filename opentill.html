Getting started

Four steps, about two minutes. Everything here is testnet play money.

  1. Connect a wallet. It'll offer to add Arc Testnet if you don't have it.
  2. Get some test USDC. The faucet at faucet.circle.com gives 1 USDC a day — enough to try everything.
  3. Open a till. Name it, add one payee at 100% for a tip jar or a few for a split, and hit open.
  4. Pay into it. Type an amount and watch the fan divide before you sign.

Pay in

Goes straight to the payees in one transaction. Nothing pools here first.

Set up a repeat

Charges on a schedule against an allowance you set. Take the allowance away and it stops — no cancellation flow, no retention offer.

You approve an allowance once. Each charge pulls from it until it runs out or you revoke it.

Open your own till

Name it, list who gets what, and share the link. Payees are fixed at creation — nobody can redirect them later, including you.
